## What changed
All 32 response points in `aqua/src/routes/intelligence.js` go through `ok()` / `fail()`. The only wire-visible change is a new `code` field on failures. Statuses, `error` text and success bodies are unchanged.

New taxonomy member: `upstream_failed` -> 502 (`ErrorCodes.UPSTREAM_FAILED`). `POST /intelligence/orchestrate` has always failed with a 502; folding it into `unavailable` (503) or `internal` (500) would have changed a status a client may branch on. Same precedent as `payload_too_large`.

- Runtime-covered: owner/validation failures, 404s, the kill switch, and the 503/404 "unavailable" paths (driven by `AQUA_PIC=off`).
- Structural only, and declared as such: the five 500 handlers and the 502 in `orchestrate`. They fire only when an engine function throws, and the orchestrate path would reach real model providers on any machine with keys, so a test must not trigger it. The test pins the mapping, not the throw.

## Finding, not changed
