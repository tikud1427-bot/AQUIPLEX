# Patch 2026-10-02 — "Where do I work?" ranked the wrong answer first

Battery: 3479 tests, 0 fail, 3 skipped. `eval:gate` exit 0 with gains.

## Cause
In `questionShape.js`, `work` is a typing cue (so excluded from `topicTerms`) but stayed in `shape.terms`, which the lexical score reads. Noun-`work` facts ("I usually do deep work in the mornings", "Neha owns the retrieval work") scored a perfect 1.0 and outranked "I run product at Nummo".

## Fix
`WORK_VERB` recognises the verb construction only (pronoun or auxiliary+pronoun before it, or `at`/`for` after it) and drops `work` from the matched terms. "Who owns the retrieval work?" and "How do I get to work?" keep it.
Tests: `src/pic/tests/workVerb.test.js` (18). Each of the three regex forms and the narrowness both bite under mutation.
